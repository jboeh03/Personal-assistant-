# Visit SOP

**Owner:** `wpc-ops` · **Status:** field procedure — read this on the truck, not at a desk

**Adopted from the owner's laminated field checklist** — his eight blocks, his sequence, his
time budgets, and his `NOTES / ITEMS TO QUOTE / FOLLOW-UP` block, which is the project-finding
mechanism the whole model depends on (`CANON.md` §2). Designed for printed + laminated use
with a dry-erase marker, one block at a time, one-handed in a driveway.

**There is no Dormant Season SOP.** The membership runs March 1 – October 31 only. There is
no scheduled service the rest of the year, so there is nothing to write a winter procedure
for (`service-agreement.md` §6.8).

---

## The two-hour cap, stated so it can't be misread

This SOP has time budgets printed on it, and that is deliberate — it is not a contradiction
of anything. `CANON.md` §3 states both halves precisely, and both are true at the same time:

- **"Up to two hours of on-site service per visit" is a scope boundary.** It is a
  contractual limit on what the customer bought (`service-agreement.md` §1), and the eight
  blocks below are how those two hours are budgeted, in the owner's own hand. Printing the
  budget and working to it is not banned — it is the product.
- **A minutes-per-visit performance target is still banned.** No average-visit-length KPI, no
  copy or habit implying the goal is finishing quickly, and no grading a real visit against
  these budgets after the fact (`operating-model.md` §7). Membership standard 1 — **never
  rush a property because another client is waiting** (`operating-model.md` §8, rule 1) — is
  unchanged, and the cap serves that rule rather than fighting it: because the customer's
  entitlement is capped at two hours, nobody is ever choosing between finishing this property
  and reaching the next one.

**In one line: the budget below is a ceiling on what the customer bought, not a stopwatch on
how he worked.** Work each block at whatever pace the property actually needs, inside the
two-hour ceiling — and if something runs past it, stop, name it, locate it, price it, and get
approval before doing it (§12 below).

> **WPC513 — Maintain everything. Deep-clean nothing unless specifically scheduled.**

## Before you leave the shop

- [ ] Pull up the property file — access notes, pets, gate code, known issues, anything
      flagged from the last visit
- [ ] Confirm access: gate code, key, or that someone will be home
- [ ] Check the weather. If it's a washout, this is a reschedule call, not a visit — see
      `service-report-templates.md` (weather-rescheduled template) and move the visit within
      the same calendar month
- [ ] Load the truck: pool net and pole, vacuum head and hose, pool brush, skimmer/pump
      basket tool (if the property has a pool), blower, hand tools, trash bags, surface
      cleaner, microfiber cloths, degreaser for the grease trap, spot weed treatment,
      watering can or hose adapter, phone charged for photos

## THE 2-HOUR IN-SEASON VISIT CHECKLIST

*Client / Property: _______________ Date: _______________ Visit: _____ of 16*

| # | Block | Window | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival + property walkthrough | 0:00–0:10 | 10 min |
| 2 | **Pool care** *(if applicable)* | 0:10–0:40 | **30 min max** |
| 3 | Outdoor kitchen + grill area *(if applicable)* | 0:40–1:00 | 20 min |
| 4 | Patio + furniture | 1:00–1:20 | 20 min |
| 5 | Property cleanup | 1:20–1:35 | 15 min |
| 6 | Trash + plants + misc. | 1:35–1:45 | 10 min |
| 7 | Flexible priority block | 1:45–1:55 | 10 min |
| 8 | Final walk + documentation | 1:55–2:00 | 5 min |

Blocks 2 and 3 only exist if the property has a pool or an outdoor kitchen. A property with
neither runs shorter — and isn't a target client for exactly that reason
(`ideal-client.md` §2).

### 1. Arrival + property walkthrough — 10 min

- [ ] Walk the outdoor living areas before touching anything
- [ ] Check for storm debris, leaves, sticks, trash, and obvious issues
- [ ] Visually check the pool area and equipment
- [ ] Check the outdoor kitchen and grill area
- [ ] Note anything unusual or requiring customer approval
- [ ] Quick before photos where useful

### 2. Pool care — 30 min max *(if the property has a pool)*

- [ ] Skim the surface
- [ ] Brush steps, corners, and selected areas
- [ ] Vacuum as needed — **stop at approximately 30 minutes of total pool time**
- [ ] Empty the skimmer basket
- [ ] Empty the pump basket when appropriate and safe
- [ ] Empty the automatic pool-vacuum debris container if applicable
- [ ] Check the displayed salt level / system indicators
- [ ] Check Inspect Cell / No Flow / other visible warnings
- [ ] Backwash if needed and appropriate for the system
- [ ] Quick visual equipment inspection

**What this block is not:** water testing, balancing, adding any chemical, filter servicing
beyond backwashing, or any guarantee of the result. Say so if it comes up —
`service-agreement.md` §5.2, §6.1.

### 3. Outdoor kitchen + grill area — 20 min *(if the property has one)*

- [ ] Wipe countertops and accessible outdoor-kitchen surfaces
- [ ] Remove light food / grease buildup
- [ ] Empty the grease trap / drip pan when needed
- [ ] Wipe the grill exterior
- [ ] Check the cooking area for excessive buildup
- [ ] Look for obvious pest / nesting activity
- [ ] Note repairs or deep-cleaning needs for the report

### 4. Patio + furniture — 20 min

- [ ] Wipe patio tables
- [ ] Wipe chairs / furniture as needed
- [ ] Straighten cushions and furniture
- [ ] Wipe frequently used surfaces
- [ ] Remove obvious cobwebs
- [ ] Light cleanup around seating areas

### 5. Property cleanup — 15 min

- [ ] Collect sticks / small branches
- [ ] Remove concentrated leaf / debris pockets
- [ ] Pick up visible trash
- [ ] Sweep / blow small designated areas where appropriate
- [ ] Check walkways around the outdoor living area

### 6. Trash + plants + misc. — 10 min

- [ ] Empty designated outdoor trash cans
- [ ] Light wipe-down of cans when needed
- [ ] Water outdoor flower pots if requested
- [ ] Handle small recurring homeowner priorities
- [ ] Check previously noted minor issues

### 7. Flexible priority block — 10 min

**The most valuable ten minutes of the visit.** Whichever the property needs most today:

- [ ] Extra pool attention
- [ ] A heavier debris pocket
- [ ] Outdoor kitchen or grill cleanup
- [ ] Furniture or patio detail
- [ ] Plants or a seasonal need
- [ ] A small approved maintenance item

### 8. Final walk + documentation — 5 min

- [ ] Final property walk
- [ ] Return gates and areas as found
- [ ] After photos
- [ ] Record completed services in the property file — see `property-record-template.md`
- [ ] Note repairs / additional-service recommendations
- [ ] Send the service report the same day — see `service-report-templates.md`

## What never appears on this SOP, on purpose

- **No minutes-per-visit performance target.** The block budgets above are a scope boundary,
  not a KPI — see the cap section at the top of this page. `CANON.md` §3,
  `operating-model.md` §7.
- **No arrival time or arrival window.** Clients are told their route day, not their hour —
  `service-agreement.md` §6.11.
- **No task treated as mandatory on every visit.** The blocks above are what's usually
  relevant, not a checklist that has to be fully checked to count as a complete visit —
  `service-catalog.md` §1.
- **No Dormant Season variant.** The membership does not run November through February.
  There is no scheduled service to write an SOP for.

> **WPC513 — Maintain everything. Deep-clean nothing unless specifically scheduled.**
