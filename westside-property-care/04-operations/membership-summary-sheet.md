## Sixteen visits. Six properties. Written down every time.

I'm on your property [Monday / Friday], twice each calendar month, **March 1
through October 31** — sixteen visits a season. That's your route day, not an arrival time.
Up to two hours on site each visit, highest-priority items first; completion of every
possible task every visit isn't guaranteed, and unused time doesn't roll over.

**You're not really buying sixteen visits — you're buying the sixteenth one:** the visit
where I already know which gate sticks, which window well fills, and what your property
needs before you've noticed it, because I wrote down the other fifteen.

What's actually covered, when it applies: pool skimmed, brushed, vacuumed, and backwashed
when the system needs it, both baskets emptied. No guarantee of water chemistry, water
clarity, equipment performance, or pool-system operation. Also covered: patio and furniture
reset, outdoor kitchen wiped down, debris cleared, pots watered, hardscape weeds
spot-treated. Not every item every visit — whatever the property actually needs that day.

**November through February: no scheduled visits, and I'm not billing you for any of them.**
Your last visit of the season tells you exactly what to expect over the winter and what the
property needs before it gets cold; your next one is the first route day in March.

## Four things worth knowing before you sign

Said now so none of these is a surprise later — each one is still available, just quoted and
scheduled separately from the membership:

- **Pool chemistry and chemicals** — not included, even though I vacuum, brush, and backwash
  the pool itself. I don't test or balance the water, and I don't guarantee its water
  chemistry, water clarity, equipment performance, or pool-system operation. Full pool service
  with chemicals runs about $121–142 a month elsewhere, and I can point you to who your
  neighbors use.
- **Lawn and landscape work** — not included. No mowing, no bed weeding, no mulch. Spot weed
  treatment in the hardscape joints and walkway cracks is included; the beds themselves are a
  quoted job.
- **Anything needing a ladder** — not included. Gutter cleaning is the common example — I
  check the discharge from the ground and send a real number the next morning if actual
  cleaning is wanted, rather than guess one in the driveway.
- **Deep cleaning the grill** — burners, grates, the inside — isn't the routine wipe-down.
  That's a separate job, from $249.

Twelve exclusions in total are in the attached agreement, §6. I'd rather you hear all of it
today than find one out in month four.

## Billing, and how to leave

Card on file, charged automatically on the schedule set when you sign, **March through
October only** — eight charges a season, none between November and February. If you're starting
after March 1, your first month is prorated by the visit — see the attached agreement §7.
**Thirty days' written notice cancels it, either direction, no penalty** — text, email, or
letter to the contact information on the attached agreement.
