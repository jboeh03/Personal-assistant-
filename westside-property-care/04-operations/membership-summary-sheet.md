## What twice a month means

I'm on your property [Monday / Friday] of the first and third week each month — that's your
route day, not an arrival time.

**April–October (Green Season):** pool skimmed and baskets emptied if you have one, patio
and furniture reset, outdoor kitchen wiped down if you have one, debris cleared, pots
watered, hardscape weeds spot-treated. Not every item every visit — whatever the property
needs that day.

**November–March (Dormant Season):** hose bibs and water lines drained and protected from
freeze, gutters checked from the ground, storm debris cleared, furniture and the grill
covered, ice managed on the patio, a full photo inspection. The March visit adds what the
property needs for spring, priced.

**Same price both seasons.** You're buying a year of coverage, not a summer of visits.

## Four things worth knowing before you sign

Said now so none of these is a surprise later — each one is still available, just quoted
and scheduled separately from the membership:

- **Pool chemistry and chemicals** — not included. I skim and empty baskets; I don't touch
  the water. Full pool service runs about $121–142 a month elsewhere, and I can point you to
  who your neighbors use.
- **Lawn and landscape work** — not included. No mowing, no bed weeding, no mulch. Spot
  weed treatment in the hardscape is included; the beds themselves are a quoted job.
- **Anything needing a ladder** — not included. Gutter cleaning is the common example — I
  look at it on site and send a real number the next morning rather than guess one in the
  driveway.
- **Deep cleaning the grill** — burners, grates, the inside — isn't the routine wipe-down.
  That's a separate job, from $249.

Twelve exclusions in total are in the attached agreement, §6. I'd rather you hear all of it
today than find one out in month four.

## Billing, and how to leave

Card on file, charged automatically on the 1st of the month, same price every month of the
year including winter. **Thirty days' written notice cancels it, either direction, no
penalty** — text, email, or letter to the contact information on the attached agreement.
