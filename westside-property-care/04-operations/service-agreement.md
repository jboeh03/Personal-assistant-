## 2. Founding Member rate

[ ] **This Member is one of the first four members of Westside Property Care** and holds the
Founding Member rate.

If checked: the price in §1 is locked for **twelve (12) months from the effective date
above** and will not increase during that period even if Westside Property Care's standard

project work or materials. What happens if the property itself changes during the lock
period is in §3. The lock is a price guarantee, not a term commitment — the cancellation
terms in §8 apply the same as to any other member.

## 3. When the property changes

The price in §1 follows the property, not the calendar. If what the property has changes —
a pool is installed, an outdoor kitchen is built, or either one is removed — the
configuration and the price change with it.

**Notice.** Member tells Company in writing (text, email, or in person at a visit) as soon
as the change happens or is scheduled. Company will also flag an obvious change noticed
during a visit and confirm it with the Member before treating it as anything more than an
observation.

**When the new price starts.** The new configuration and price take effect on the **1st of
the next full billing month** following notice or confirmation — never mid-month and never
retroactively.

**If this Member holds the Founding Member rate (§2).** The lock protects this Member's
price from Westside Property Care's own rate increases; it does not freeze the property at
the configuration it had on the day it was signed. If a feature is **added**, the added
portion is billed at the standard rate in effect on the date it is added, and the new
combined total becomes the locked amount for the remainder of the twelve months. If a
feature is **removed**, the price drops to match the new configuration, and that lower
amount is what stays locked for the remainder of the twelve months. Either way, the
remaining lock period does not restart and does not extend — it still ends twelve months
from the effective date in the header, regardless of how many times the configuration
changes in between.

## 4. Term and renewal

This agreement begins on the effective date and continues **month to month**. There is no
fixed term. It renews automatically each month unless ended under §8.

## 5. What this includes

Two scheduled visits per calendar month, on the assigned route day, to the outdoor living
area of the property identified at the initial walkthrough and recorded in the property file
— the patio, the pool and pool deck if the property has one, the outdoor kitchen if the
property has one, the adjoining hardscape, and the walkways serving those areas.

Not every task happens on every visit. Each visit is spent on what the property actually
needs that day: a walk-through before starting and again before leaving, and — as
applicable to the property — pool-area care, patio and furniture care, outdoor-kitchen care,
and debris and grounds care within the outdoor living area. The full task lists for each
season are in `00-model/service-catalog.md` §§2–3, incorporated into this agreement by
reference.

**Green Season, April through October,** and **Dormant Season, November through March,** are
two different scopes of work at one flat price. Dormant Season shifts the work from
presentation to protection: freeze protection on hose bibs and outdoor kitchen water lines,
a ground-level gutter and downspout check, storm debris, furniture and grill covering and
storage, ice management on the outdoor living hardscape, and a full exterior inspection with
a photo report, including spring-prep recommendations in March.

**The price in §1 does not change between seasons.** It is the same amount in January as it
is in July.

## 6. What this does not include

**The principle behind this section: the membership is built to find additional work on the
property, not to absorb it.** Everything below is not part of the membership. Most of it can
still be done — as a separately quoted and separately priced project. Ask.

**6.1 — Pool.** Membership Pool Care is skimming the surface, emptying the skimmer and pump
baskets, and clearing debris from the pool deck and surround, on the two scheduled visits per
month. **It is not pool service.** Not included: water chemistry testing or balancing;
adding chlorine, shock, algaecide, or any chemical; vacuuming the pool; brushing walls or
steps; cleaning, backwashing, or servicing the filter; pump, heater, or equipment repair;
opening or closing the pool for the season; cover installation or removal; leak detection;
and any work on or in the pool below the water surface. **Westside Property Care does not
maintain pool water quality and is not responsible for water condition, clarity, or
chemistry.**

**6.2 — Lawn and landscape.** Not included, of any kind: mowing, trimming, edging, blowing of
lawn areas, fertilization, aeration, overseeding, weed-and-feed, pruning, shrub or tree
trimming, planting, mulching, bed edging, bed cleanout, or landscape installation. Weed
treatment under the membership is limited to spot treatment of visible weeds in hardscape
joints, walkway cracks, and along the edges of beds bordering the outdoor living area.
Weeding inside planting beds is a quoted project.

**6.3 — Boundary of the property.** The membership covers only the exterior outdoor living
area identified and agreed at the walkthrough and recorded in the property file. Not
included: any interior space, including garages, basements, enclosed porches, and sunrooms;
the front of the property except the walkway serving the outdoor living area; lawn areas;
wooded or unmaintained portions of the lot; outbuildings; and any area not identified at the
walkthrough.

**6.4 — Height and equipment.** No work is performed from a ladder, from a roof, or above
roof line. Gutter and downspout inspection is done from the ground, visually. Work requiring
a pressure washer, a ladder, a chainsaw, powered digging equipment, a trailer, or any
chemical beyond routine surface cleaner and spot weed treatment falls outside the membership
and is quoted separately.

**6.5 — Deep cleaning.** Grill and outdoor kitchen care under the membership is emptying the
grease trap and drip tray and wiping down exterior surfaces and countertops. Not included:
cleaning of burners, grates, flavorizer bars, heat shields, interiors, hoods, or vents;
degreasing; and any disassembly of cooking equipment. That is a grill and outdoor kitchen
deep clean, quoted separately (from $249 — see `estimate-templates.md`). Pressure washing of
any surface is not included and is quoted separately.

**6.6 — Debris and waste volume.** Included: hand-collected loose leaves, sticks, and debris
from the outdoor living area, transferred to the Member's own designated waste containers, up
to the capacity of those containers. Not included: debris exceeding container capacity;
removal of any waste from the property; moving containers to or from the curb; hauling;
disposal; and dump fees. Large-volume debris removal and hauling are quoted, with disposal
billed at cost plus 25%.

**6.7 — Storm response.** Included: clearing storm debris from the outdoor living area on the
next scheduled visit, by hand, within the limits of §6.6. Not included: any storm response
requiring a saw, a trailer, more than one trip, or more than the Member's container capacity;
tree or large-limb removal; and structural or property damage repair. **Westside Property
Care is not an emergency service and does not offer guaranteed response times.**

**6.8 — Snow and ice.** Included: salting and clearing ice from the outdoor living hardscape
— patio, pool deck, and the walkway serving the outdoor living area — on scheduled visits
only. Not included: driveway or public sidewalk clearing; plowing; shoveling of any area
outside the outdoor living hardscape; and any snow or ice response between scheduled visits.

**6.9 — Repairs, parts, and trades.** Westside Property Care does not perform repairs,
replace parts, or install, alter, or remove any fixed element of the property under the
membership. Anything requiring a replacement part, a permit, or a licensed trade —
electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC, structural, or pool equipment — is quoted separately and may
be referred to a licensed contractor. Replacement parts and materials are always billed
separately, at cost plus 25% (§10).

**6.10 — Watering and plant care.** Included: watering of flower pots and container plantings
in the outdoor living area, on scheduled visits. Not included: lawn or garden bed irrigation;
irrigation system operation, repair, or winterization; and plant care between scheduled
visits, including vacation plant watering.

**6.11 — Visit frequency and scheduling.** The membership is two scheduled visits per
calendar month, on the assigned route day. Not included: additional visits; on-demand
visits; visits on a specific requested date; guaranteed arrival times; and weekend service.
Visits may be rescheduled for weather or safety — see §11.

**6.12 — Access, pets, and third parties.** See §9.

## 8. Cancellation

Either party may end this agreement with **thirty (30) days' written notice** — by text,
email, or letter, to the contact information on this page. Service and billing continue
through the end of the paid month. There is no cancellation fee. The Founding Member price
lock in §2 does not change these terms — it is a price guarantee, not a term commitment.

## 9. Property access, pets, and third parties

Member is responsible for providing safe access to the serviced area on the assigned route
day — including gate codes or keys — and for securing pets during the visit. Westside
Property Care is not responsible for work that cannot be performed because access was
unavailable, and does not assume responsibility for conditions created by other contractors,
household members, or third parties on the property. A visit that cannot be performed for
lack of access is not refunded and is not made up outside the scheduled route days.

## 10. Materials and parts

Materials, replacement parts, and disposal used on project work are billed separately from
labor, at **cost plus 25%**, and itemized on the estimate before the work starts and on the
invoice after. Routine consumables used on a membership visit — surface cleaner, trash bags,
spot weed treatment, ice melt — are covered by the membership and are never billed
separately.

## 11. Weather and rescheduling

A visit may be moved for weather or safety. A rescheduled visit is performed within the same
calendar month where possible. Nothing about billing in §7 changes because a visit was
rescheduled.

## 12. Work found during a visit that isn't included

If Westside Property Care finds work during a scheduled visit that is outside the scope in
§5 and §6, it will be named, located, and priced — or, if it needs a closer look, the Member
will be told that plainly — before anything is done. **Nothing outside this agreement's
scope is performed, or billed, without the Member's agreement first.** Declining a
recommendation has no effect on the membership, the price, or future visits.

## 16. What Westside Property Care is not

Not a pool service, a lawn or landscape company, a snow contractor, an emergency or on-call
service, or a repair or handyman service. §6 states each of those boundaries as binding
terms of this agreement, not as marketing description.
