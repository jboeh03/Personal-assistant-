## 1. Membership and scheduled service

**$279 per month** includes **two scheduled property-care visits each month**, on the
assigned route day, with **up to two hours of on-site service per visit**. The service is
designed to keep the outdoor living areas identified at the initial walkthrough and recorded
in the property file routinely maintained, clean, functional, and ready to enjoy.

**Unused service time does not roll over.** A visit that needs less than two hours does not
bank the difference for a future visit, and a visit that could use more than two hours does
not borrow time from one.

**One price.** $279 a month is the entire membership price — it is the same whether the
property has a pool, an outdoor kitchen, both, or neither. There is no configuration table
and no add-on price for either feature.

## 2. Founding Member rate

**Founding Member status is set by one fact: the date this Member signs this agreement, entered
under Signatures below.** Any Member who signs on or before **February 28, 2027** is a Founding
Member. There is no limit on how many Members hold this rate — it applies whether this is the
first service agreement WPC513 has signed or the sixth. The test is the **signature date**, not
the effective date above and not the date of the first visit: a Member who signs in February
2027 for a March 1 start still qualifies. A Member who signs after February 28, 2027 is not a
Founding Member and pays WPC513's standard rate on standard terms; the remainder of this section
does not apply to that Member.

$279 is locked for this Member through October 31, 2027 — covering the pro-rated remainder of the
2026 Service Season (§7, Billing) plus the entirety of the 2027 Service Season (March 1 –
October 31, 2027) — and will not increase during that span even if WPC513's standard pricing
changes generally. The lock covers the membership price only; it does not cover project work or
materials (§10). It does not extend into 2028 or any season after 2027. **The lock is a price
guarantee, not a term commitment** — the cancellation terms in §8 apply the same as to any other
member.

## 3. Property changes

The price in §1 does not change based on what the property has, and it does not move if the
property changes after signing — a pool and an outdoor kitchen cost the same as a plain
patio, on the day this agreement is signed and on every day after.

**Notice.** If the outdoor living area changes materially after signing — a pool is
installed or removed, an outdoor kitchen is built or removed, or the serviced area otherwise
changes significantly — Member tells WPC513 in writing (text, email, or in person at a visit)
as soon as the change happens or is scheduled. WPC513 will also flag an obvious change
noticed during a visit and confirm it with Member before treating it as anything more than an
observation.

**Why notice still matters even though price doesn't.** A material change affects how the
two-hour visit is spent (§5.4, Service priorities) and it is recorded in the property file so
the visit plan matches the property as it actually is. It does not, on its own, change what
this Member is billed.

## 4. Term, season, and renewal

This agreement begins on the effective date above and covers the Service Season identified —
**March 1 through October 31**. It renews automatically for each subsequent Service Season on
the same dates unless ended under §8.

**There is no scheduled service and no membership charge between November 1 and the last day
of February.** The card on file (§7) stays on file, unused, during that period. WPC513 will
confirm re-enrollment with Member in writing before the next season resumes. This agreement
remains in effect across the off-season unless a party ends it under §8.

## 5. What this includes

Not every item happens on every visit. Each visit starts and ends with a walk of the serviced
area, and in between covers whatever the property actually needs that day — see §5.4.
**Schedule A, attached to and made part of this agreement, is the same in-season visit
checklist used on site**, and this section summarizes it.

**5.1 — Routine property care.** May include: general outdoor-living-area checks; light
debris and leaf cleanup in designated areas; patio and outdoor-space tidying; outdoor
countertop and furniture wipe-down; outdoor trash-can emptying and light wipe-down; outdoor
kitchen upkeep; grill exterior and cooking-area maintenance; grease-trap or drip-pan emptying
when appropriate; collection of sticks and small storm debris; watering outdoor flower pots
when requested; basic visual checks of outdoor equipment and amenities; and notification of
observed maintenance concerns. The specific area covered is identified and agreed at the
initial walkthrough and recorded in the property file (§6.3).

**5.2 — Basic pool care.** When applicable, may include: surface skimming; brushing steps,
corners, and selected areas; vacuuming for up to approximately 30 minutes per visit; emptying
skimmer and pump baskets when safely accessible; emptying automatic pool-vacuum debris
containers when applicable; backwashing when appropriate and compatible with the system;
visual equipment checks; checking displayed salt levels or system indicators; and
notification of visible equipment warnings.

> **WPC513 does not guarantee water chemistry, water clarity, equipment performance, or
> pool-system operation unless separately contracted.**

**5.3 — Outdoor kitchen and grill care.** Wiping countertops and accessible surfaces; light
cleaning around cooking equipment; removal of accessible grease or food residue; grease-trap
or drip-pan emptying when appropriate; general visual inspection. **Professional grill deep
cleaning, repairs, replacement parts, restoration, and equipment disassembly are not
included** and may be quoted separately (§6.5).

**5.4 — Service priorities.** Member may identify preferred recurring priorities. Because
each visit is limited to approximately two hours, **WPC513 will use reasonable judgment to
address the highest-priority maintenance items first. Completion of every possible task
during every visit is not guaranteed.**

## 6. What this does not include

**The principle behind this section, in WPC513's own words: maintain everything, deep-clean
nothing unless specifically scheduled.** Everything below is not part of the membership. Most
of it can still be done — as a separately quoted and separately priced project. Ask.

**6.1 — Pool.** Pool care under the membership is the physical maintenance described in
§5.2 — skimming, brushing, vacuuming for up to approximately 30 minutes per visit, emptying
the skimmer and pump baskets and the automatic pool-vacuum debris container when applicable,
backwashing when appropriate and compatible with the system, and checking displayed salt
levels and system indicators. **It is not pool service.** Not included: water chemistry
testing or balancing of any kind; adding chlorine, shock, algaecide, stabilizer, acid, salt,
or any other chemical to the water; filter servicing beyond backwashing a system designed to
be backwashed — cleaning, disassembly, cartridge replacement, or DE charging; pump, heater,
salt-cell, automation, or plumbing repair, replacement, or adjustment beyond what a displayed
indicator can be read from; opening or closing the pool for the season, cover installation or
removal, and winterization; and leak detection, liner or surface repair, tile and waterline
scale removal, acid washing, and any work below the water surface other than the vacuuming
and brushing described in §5.2.

> **WPC513 does not guarantee water chemistry, water clarity, equipment performance, or
> pool-system operation unless separately contracted.**

**6.2 — Lawn and landscape.** Not included, of any kind: mowing, trimming, edging, blowing of
lawn areas, fertilization, aeration, overseeding, weed-and-feed, pruning, shrub or tree
trimming, planting, mulching, bed edging, bed cleanout, or landscape installation. Weed
treatment under the membership is limited to spot treatment of visible weeds in hardscape
joints, walkway cracks, and along the edges of beds bordering the outdoor living area.
Weeding inside planting beds is a quoted project. **Regulated pesticide or
chemical-application services are excluded** unless WPC513 holds any licensing legally
required (§10).

**6.3 — Boundary of the property.** The membership covers only the exterior outdoor living
area identified and agreed at the walkthrough and recorded in the property file. Not
included: any interior space, including garages, basements, enclosed porches, and sunrooms;
the front of the property except the walkway serving the outdoor living area; lawn areas;
wooded or unmaintained portions of the lot; outbuildings; and any area not identified at the
walkthrough.

**6.4 — Height and equipment.** No work is performed from a ladder, from a roof, or above
roof line. Gutter and downspout inspection is done from the ground, visually. Work requiring
a pressure washer, a ladder, a chainsaw, powered digging equipment, a trailer, or any
chemical beyond routine surface cleaner and spot weed treatment falls outside the membership
and is quoted separately.

**6.5 — Deep cleaning.** Grill and outdoor kitchen care under the membership is wiping
countertops and accessible surfaces, light cleaning around cooking equipment, removal of
accessible grease or food residue, grease-trap or drip-pan emptying when appropriate, and
general visual inspection. **Professional grill deep cleaning, repairs, replacement parts,
restoration, and equipment disassembly are not included** and may be quoted separately.
Specifically not included: cleaning of burners, grates, flavorizer bars, heat shields,
interiors, hoods, or vents; degreasing; and any disassembly of cooking equipment. That is a
grill and outdoor kitchen deep clean, quoted separately as a written estimate, from $249.
**Pressure washing of any surface is not included** and is quoted separately.

**6.6 — Debris and waste volume.** Included: hand-collected loose leaves, sticks, and debris
from the outdoor living area, transferred to Member's own designated waste containers, up to
the capacity of those containers, and within the two-hour scheduled visit. Not included:
debris exceeding container capacity; debris requiring more time than the visit allows;
removal of any waste from the property; moving containers to or from the curb; hauling;
disposal; and dump fees. Large-volume debris removal and hauling are quoted, with disposal
billed at cost plus 25%.

**6.7 — Storm response.** Included: clearing storm debris from the outdoor living area on the
next scheduled visit, by hand, within the limits of §6.6. Not included: any storm response
requiring a saw, a trailer, more than one trip, or more than Member's container capacity;
tree or large-limb removal; and structural or property damage repair. **WPC513 is not an
emergency service and does not offer guaranteed response times.**

**6.8 — Off-season.** The membership runs **March 1 through October 31 only.** During
November, December, January, and February there are **no scheduled visits, no property
checks, no storm response, no freeze protection, and no snow or ice service of any kind — and
no membership charge.** Any work performed during those months is a separately arranged and
separately quoted project. A separate, optional winter service may be offered from time to
time; it is never part of this agreement and is never included unless a separate written
agreement is signed for it.

**6.9 — Repairs, parts, and trades.** WPC513 does not perform repairs, replace parts, or
install, alter, or remove any fixed element of the property under the membership. Anything
requiring a replacement part, a permit, or a licensed trade — electrical, plumbing, gas,
HVAC, structural, or pool equipment — is quoted separately and may be referred to a licensed
contractor. Replacement parts and materials are always billed separately, at cost plus 25%
(§10).

**6.10 — Watering and plant care.** Included: watering of flower pots and container plantings
in the outdoor living area, on scheduled visits, when requested. Not included: lawn or garden
bed irrigation; irrigation system operation, repair, or winterization; and plant care between
scheduled visits, including vacation plant watering.

**6.11 — Visit frequency, scheduling, and the two-hour cap.** The membership is two scheduled
visits per calendar month, March through October, on the assigned route day. **Each visit
includes up to two hours of on-site service. Unused service time does not roll over.** Not
included: additional visits; on-demand visits; visits on a specific requested date;
guaranteed arrival times; weekend service; and any accumulation or carry-forward of unused
time. Arrival times may vary — see §11.

**6.12 — Access, pets, and third parties.** See §9.

## 8. Cancellation

Either party may end this agreement with **thirty (30) days' written notice** — by text,
email, or letter, to the contact information on this page. Amounts already earned for
completed service periods remain due. WPC513 may discontinue service for unsafe working
conditions, repeated nonpayment, abusive conduct, unreasonable service expectations, or
conditions outside the agreed scope. There is no cancellation fee. The Founding Member price
lock in §2 does not change these terms — it is a price guarantee, not a term commitment.

## 9. Property access, pets, and third parties

Member provides reasonable access including gates, outdoor kitchens, pool equipment,
electrical outlets, and water sources, on the assigned route day. **Pets should be secured
when necessary for safe service.** WPC513 is not responsible for work that cannot be
performed because access was unavailable, and does not assume responsibility for conditions
created by other contractors, household members, or third parties on the property. A visit
that cannot be performed for lack of access is not refunded and is not made up outside the
scheduled route days.

## 10. Materials, chemicals, and parts

The monthly membership primarily covers **scheduled labor and routine care.** Replacement
parts, specialty cleaners, pool chemicals, salt, filters, repair materials, landscaping
materials, and other consumables are additional unless specifically included, and are billed
at **cost plus 25%**, itemized on the estimate before the work starts and on the invoice
after. **Regulated pesticide or chemical-application services are excluded unless WPC513
holds any licensing legally required.** Routine consumables used on a membership visit —
surface cleaner, trash bags, spot weed treatment — are covered by the membership and are
never billed separately.

## 11. Scheduling and weather

Service is generally provided twice per month from March through October on an approximate
recurring schedule. Arrival times may vary because of weather, prior appointments, traffic,
emergencies, or property conditions. **Weather-related visits may be moved to the next
reasonably available service date**, within the same calendar month where possible. Nothing
about billing in §7 changes because a visit was rescheduled.

## 12. Additional work

**The principle behind this section: the membership is built to find additional work on the
property, not to absorb it.** **Work beyond the scheduled service time or outside routine
service requires Member approval before additional billable work is performed.** If WPC513
finds work during a scheduled visit that is outside the scope in §5 and §6, it will be named,
located, and priced — or, if it needs a closer look, Member will be told that plainly —
before anything is done. Examples: professional grill deep cleaning, pressure washing,
extensive storm or leaf cleanup, equipment or pool repairs, replacement parts, landscaping
projects, hauling, specialty cleaning, and significant seasonal opening or closing work.
**Nothing outside this agreement's scope is performed, or billed, without Member's agreement
first.** Declining a recommendation has no effect on the membership, the price, or future
visits.

## 13. Liability and insurance

WPC513 carries general liability insurance: [carrier name — pending], policy number
[pending], in force as of [date — pending]. A certificate is available on request.

**WPC513 is not responsible for pre-existing damage, deterioration, hidden defects,

Not a pool service, a lawn or landscape company, a snow contractor, an emergency or on-call
service, a repair or handyman service, or a year-round service. §6 states each of those
boundaries as binding terms of this agreement, not as marketing description. Service runs
March 1 – October 31 only — see §4.

## Schedule A — In-Season Visit Checklist

Attached to and made part of this agreement. §5 refers to this schedule; this schedule does
not change the price in §1, the exclusions in §6, or any other term above.

**The rule that governs this whole schedule: not every item happens on every visit.** Each
visit is spent on what the property actually needs that day, within the budget below.

> **WPC513 — Maintain everything. Deep-clean nothing unless specifically scheduled.**

| # | Block | Window | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival + property walkthrough | 0:00–0:10 | 10 min |
| 2 | Pool care *(if applicable)* | 0:10–0:40 | 30 min max |
| 3 | Outdoor kitchen + grill area *(if applicable)* | 0:40–1:00 | 20 min |
| 4 | Patio + furniture | 1:00–1:20 | 20 min |
| 5 | Property cleanup | 1:20–1:35 | 15 min |
| 6 | Trash + plants + misc. | 1:35–1:45 | 10 min |
| 7 | Flexible priority block | 1:45–1:55 | 10 min |
| 8 | Final walk + documentation | 1:55–2:00 | 5 min |

**1. Arrival + property walkthrough.** Walk the outdoor living areas · check storm debris,
leaves, sticks, trash, and obvious issues · visually check the pool area and equipment ·
check the outdoor kitchen and grill area · note anything unusual or requiring Member approval
· quick before photos where useful.

**2. Pool care.** Skim the surface · brush steps, corners, and selected areas · vacuum as
needed, stopping at approximately 30 minutes of total pool time · empty the skimmer basket ·
empty the pump basket when appropriate and safe · empty the automatic pool-vacuum debris
container if applicable · check the displayed salt level and system indicators · check
Inspect Cell / No Flow / other visible warnings · backwash if needed and appropriate for the
system · quick visual equipment inspection.

**3. Outdoor kitchen + grill area.** Wipe countertops and accessible outdoor-kitchen surfaces
· remove light food and grease buildup · empty the grease trap or drip pan when needed · wipe
the grill exterior · check the cooking area for excessive buildup · look for obvious pest or
nesting activity · note repairs or deep-cleaning needs.

**4. Patio + furniture.** Wipe patio tables · wipe chairs and furniture as needed · straighten
cushions and furniture · wipe frequently used surfaces · remove obvious cobwebs · light
cleanup around seating areas.

**5. Property cleanup.** Collect sticks and small branches · remove concentrated leaf and
debris pockets · pick up visible trash · sweep or blow small designated areas where
appropriate · check walkways around the outdoor living area.

**6. Trash + plants + misc.** Empty designated outdoor trash cans · light wipe-down of cans
when needed · water outdoor flower pots if requested · handle small recurring homeowner
priorities · check previously noted minor issues.

**7. Flexible priority block.** Whichever the property needs most that day: extra pool
attention · a heavier debris pocket · outdoor kitchen or grill cleanup · furniture or patio
detail · plants or a seasonal need · a small approved maintenance item.

**8. Final walk + documentation.** Final property walk · return gates and areas as found ·
after photos · record completed services · note repairs and additional-service
recommendations · send a short service summary to Member the same day.

**NOTES / ITEMS TO QUOTE / FOLLOW-UP** — recorded at the foot of every visit's checklist and
carried into the service report and the property file. This is where the next project gets
found, named, and priced.
