# Human Checklist

Everything here requires a person. No agent can do any of it, and several items have
external lead times that will gate the launch no matter how fast the documents come
together. **Ordered by lead time, longest first.**

Owner is Jeff's brother unless noted. Fill in dates before starting — an item without a date
is an item that slips.

---

## Long lead time — start on day 1

### 1. Google Business Profile
**Lead time: days to several weeks** (postcard verification). This is the single longest
pole in the whole project.

- [ ] Create the profile at google.com/business — owner: ______ — started: ______
- [ ] Request verification immediately; do not wait for the website to be finished
- [ ] Primary category and services: use `03-marketing/google-business-profile.md`
- [ ] Service-area business (no storefront) — set the area to the qualified geography in
      `00-model/ideal-client.md`, **not** all of Cincinnati
- [ ] Postcard arrived and code entered — date: ______

A verified GBP is what makes him findable by someone who heard his name and is checking he
is real. It matters more than the website's search ranking.

### 2. LLC and EIN
**Lead time: 1–7 days.** Blocks the bank account, which blocks Stripe.

- [ ] Register the LLC with the Ohio Secretary of State — date filed: ______
- [ ] Confirm the legal name matches what goes on the agreement and the invoices
- [ ] Get the EIN from irs.gov (free, immediate online)
- [ ] Decide the customer-facing name: "Westside Property Care" vs "WPC513" — see
      `01-brand/brand-brief.md`. Use one consistently everywhere.

### 3. General liability insurance
**Lead time: 2–10 days.** **Blocks the first paid visit. Do not skip this.**

- [ ] Get quotes for $1M general liability — he is on other people's property, near pools,
      handling their equipment
- [ ] **Tell the carrier exactly what he now does to the pool.** This changed and it matters.
      The scope he is publishing includes **vacuuming, brushing, emptying the skimmer and pump
      baskets and the auto-vacuum canister, backwashing, and reading salt-cell and system
      indicators.** That is pool-*system* operation. The earlier version of this business
      excluded nearly all of it, so a quote given against the old description does not cover
      the new one. See `00-model/decisions.md` R-6 — it is marked as blocking the first paid
      visit for this reason.
- [ ] Ask specifically whether backwashing and salt-cell work are excluded, in writing
- [ ] Confirm whether the vehicle needs commercial auto coverage
- [ ] Policy in force — date: ______ — carrier: ______

## Medium lead time — start day 2

### 4. Business bank account
- [ ] Open with the LLC documents and EIN — date: ______
- [ ] Keep it completely separate from personal. Every dollar in and out goes through it.

### 5. Payment processing
- [ ] Stripe account (or Jobber / Housecall Pro if he wants scheduling and invoicing
      bundled — see `04-operations/tooling.md` for the tradeoff)
- [ ] Set up **one** recurring product: **$279/month**. There is no tier and no add-on price.
- [ ] Set the billing schedule to the **March–October season only** — eight charges a year,
      not twelve. Confirm the subscription pauses automatically on November 1 rather than

chase, and the whole model stops working.

## The quality constraint — schedule it, don't leave it to chance

### 8. Photography of real completed work
**No agent can produce this, and it is the single biggest lever on how good the site looks.**

Stock photos will actively destroy the premium positioning — a homeowner in Green Township
can tell instantly, and it makes everything else on the page less believable.

Shoot on a bright overcast day, phone camera is fine, landscape orientation:

- [ ] A pool skimmed and clean, baskets emptied, area tidy
- [ ] An outdoor kitchen / grill area before and after
- [ ] A patio with furniture wiped down and hard surfaces blown clean
- [ ] A wide shot of a whole outdoor living area looking genuinely ready to use
- [ ] Him working — hands, tools, actual effort. Not posed, no crossed arms
- [ ] One clean shot of the truck and equipment
- [ ] A dormant-season shot when the season allows (furniture covered, gutters clear)

Real before/afters are worth more than any copy on the site. See the image slots in
`02-website/site-spec.md` for exactly what each one needs to show.
