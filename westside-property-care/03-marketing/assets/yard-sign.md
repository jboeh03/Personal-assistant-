## Placement and permission — read before ordering

**This sign only goes on the property of an existing, consenting client.** It is not a stake
driven into a vacant lot or a public right-of-way, and it is not placed at a prospect's home. Two
things follow:

1. **Ask the client directly, in plain terms**, before ordering — something like: *"Would you be
   willing to let me put a small sign at the curb? It doesn't say your name or your address, and
   I'll take it down whenever you want."* Get a yes before the sign exists, not after.
2. **The sign carries no identifying information about the client** — no name, no house number,
   no unit. `voice.md`'s mechanics table is explicit: street name only in anything public, never a
   full client address. A sign standing at a curb doesn't need a street name printed on it at all;
   its context *is* the address.

### Why this asset exists at all, given `brand-brief.md` §7 says a yard sign's job is recognition, not sale

A yard sign standing at a real, already-serviced property does something a cold door hanger
can't: it proves the claim on the sign is true, continuously, to the exact audience most likely
to qualify — the client's own neighbors, who by definition pass the 15-minute route test already
(`operating-model.md` §4). This is the referral mechanic in `neighborhood-campaign.md` §3 made
passive and permanent for as long as the client keeps it up.

## Dimensions and production

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Finished size | 18" × 24", single-sided — the standard residential yard-sign size, legible from the street without dominating a client's yard |
| Material | 4mm corrugated plastic (coroplast), UV-resistant, waterproof — standard for outdoor political- and real-estate-style signage and built to survive a full season outside |
| Bleed | 0.25" on all sides as a safe default — vendor bleed requirements for coroplast range from 0.125" to 0.5"; confirm the exact figure with the chosen printer before finalizing the file, and use the more generous number if there's any doubt |
| Mounting | Standard H-stake, oriented so the coroplast flutes run vertical (required for the stake to hold rigidly) — one stake is sufficient at this size |
| Color | `--paper` ground, `--ink` text and rule, WPC513 set in Plex Mono per the mark spec in `visual-direction.md` §7 — no gradient, no photo background, nothing that reads as a real-estate sign template |
| Print run | One per consenting client, ordered only after permission is confirmed — this is not a batch asset |
| Typography | Wordmark set per `visual-direction.md` §7's lockup exactly — Fraunces 600 with `WONK 1` permitted here specifically (the one screen-and-print exception named in `visual-direction.md` §3.1, "the wordmark on the truck door and the yard sign") |
