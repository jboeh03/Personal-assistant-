# Message Drafts — Meeting Ask

Two versions, warm and collaborative. Send the text first to get the yes and a date; send the fuller version (text or email) once the time is set, so she can add her items before you sit down.

---

## Version 1 — Short text (the ask)

> Hey [Name] — hope your week's going well. Could we grab an hour together sometime in the next week or two, coffee or wherever's easy? I'd love to knock out the last of the house-sale loose ends, lock in the boys' calendar and fall activities through the end of the year, and touch base on a few bigger-picture things like housing plans. Honestly, things feel like they're going really well with the boys and I want to keep it that way. I'll send a list of topics ahead of time so nothing's a surprise — and add anything you want to it. What days look good for you?

---

## Version 2 — Full agenda share (after a time is set)

> Thanks for making time on [day]. Here's everything on my list — nothing heavy, mostly closing loops and getting the rest of the year on the calendar. Add anything I'm missing and we'll get through it all:
>
> **House sale wrap-up** — I cancelled the insurance and have the tax docs handled on my end; I'll bring a recap. Mainly I want to make sure you were fully covered for the expenses leading up to the sale, and double-check every utility is paid off and nothing's still auto-charging either of us.
>
> **The documents box** — the one from the office with the boys' birth certificates and Social Security cards. Has it turned up in the move? If not, no stress — let's just make a plan.
>
> **Boys' schedule** — finalize the calendar and our cadence through the end of the year, and confirm the holiday schedule so we can both plan around it.
>
> **Fall/winter activities** — basketball at the Y and possibly Soccer Shots or GA Soccer for Archie, flag football for Teddy, and the Lexie vs. Goddard question for fall/winter. Want to pick together and sort out registration and costs.
>
> **Car insurance** — cancel or switch the payment over, whichever makes sense.
>
> **Legal** — quick update on where things stand and what's next, so neither of us is guessing.
>
> **The boys, big picture** — things honestly seem to be going really well. I'd love to compare notes on friends, trips, overnights, and general parameters, and talk about anything that would make it even easier on them. Also open to any Archie pro tips you've picked up.
>
> **Housing** — my general thinking on renting vs. buying and rough timeline, would love to hear yours, and how we want to talk to the boys about it when the time comes.
>
> See you [day] — thanks. 🙏

---

## Notes on the drafts

- **[Name]** and **[day]** are placeholders — fill in before sending.
- Version 1 deliberately leads with logistics and only lightly touches "bigger picture" — the full weight of the list lands after she's already said yes to meeting, which keeps the ask easy to accept.
- The line "things feel like they're going really well with the boys" appears in both on purpose: it frames the whole meeting as maintaining something good, not fixing something broken.
- Money items are framed as making sure *she* was covered and that *neither of you* is still being charged — cooperative, not an audit.
- If she replies to Version 2 with answers to the quick items (documents box, car insurance) before the meeting, great — cross them off and the meeting gets shorter. That's a win, not a derailment.
