# Co-Parenting Logistics Meeting — Agenda

**Format:** In person, 60–90 minutes (target ~75)
**Goal:** Close out house-sale loose ends, lock the boys' calendar through the end of the year, and align on the bigger-picture items (legal, housing, how to keep things easy on the boys).

**How to run it:** Quick administrative closeouts first (they build momentum and get real "dones" on the board), the boys in the middle when energy is best, forward-looking items after that, and 5 minutes at the end to capture who's doing what. If a topic starts running long, park it, note it, and keep moving — anything unfinished becomes the top of the next conversation.

---

## Part 1 — Closeouts (~25 min)

### 1. House sale wrap-up (10 min)
- Recap of what's done on my side:
  - [x] Insurance cancelled
  - [x] Tax docs handled for myself — I'll walk through what's relevant to you and follow up with anything you need for your filing
- Confirm **you were fully covered for expenses leading up to the sale** — anything outstanding I owe you or we need to true up?
- Confirm **all utilities are paid in full** (walk the list: power, gas, water/sewer, trash, internet)
- Confirm **no recurring charges are still hitting either of us** tied to the house (lawn, pest, security/alarm, streaming tied to the house, HOA, anything on autopay)
- I'll send a written recap after the meeting so we both have the same record

### 2. Important documents from the move (5 min)
- The box from the office — birth certificates, Social Security cards, etc. **Has it turned up?**
- If yes: agree where the originals live and who holds what (or copies for both)
- If no: agree on a search plan and a date to start replacements so it doesn't drift

### 3. Car insurance (5 min)
- Decide: cancel, or keep and switch payment/ownership of the policy?
- If switching: whose card/account, and effective date
- Confirm nothing else is auto-paying off the old shared account

### 4. Legal update (5 min)
- Where things stand, expected timeline
- Anything either of us needs to provide or sign next
- Anything we want to align on before it goes through lawyers (cheaper and easier to agree here first)

---

## Part 2 — The boys (~30 min)

### 5. Schedule & cadence — finalize through end of year (15 min)
- Updated weekly schedule/cadence — what's working, what needs adjusting
- **Finalize the calendar through Dec 31** — exchanges, school breaks, teacher workdays
- **Holiday schedule confirmation** — Thanksgiving, Christmas/winter break, New Year's (who has which days, travel windows, and how we handle family events on the other's time)
- Agree on the shared calendar as the single source of truth and who enters what

### 6. Fall/winter activities (10 min)
- **Archie:** basketball at the Y? Soccer Shots? Try GA Soccer?
  - If multiple: which combination is realistic without overloading him
- **Teddy:** flag football?
- **Lexie or Goddard for fall/winter** — decide, and note enrollment deadlines
- For whatever we pick: who registers, who pays / how we split, who covers practices vs. games on which days, gear

### 7. Big picture — how it's going for them (5–15 min, flex)
Things seem to be going well — this is the "keep it that way" conversation:
- Shared parameters we're both comfortable with: friends outside school, trips, overnights
- What would make things **better and easier on the boys** — anything either of us is seeing (moods, sleep, questions they're asking)
- **Pro tips for Archie** — what's working at each house worth copying
- Agree we flag concerns to each other early and directly

---

## Part 3 — Looking ahead (~15 min)

### 8. Housing plans (10 min)
- General timeline for renting vs. buying (both sides, to whatever extent each of us knows)
- **How and when we talk to the boys about it** — agree on consistent, low-key framing so they hear the same thing from both of us
- Practical bits: staying in the same school zone / activity radius, how a move week would work with the schedule

### 9. Wrap-up (5 min)
- Read back the action-item list — each item has an owner and a date
- Anything either of us wants on the list for next time
- Set the next check-in (suggest a standing ~monthly logistics catch-up, even 30 min, so items never pile up this long again)

## Facilitator notes (for you, not shared)

- Open by naming what's true: things are going well with the boys. It sets the frame that this meeting is maintenance, not conflict.
- The money items (expense coverage, utilities, recap) land best as *"I want to make sure you're whole and nothing's still charging either of us"* — you offering completeness, not requesting an accounting.
- The documents box: ask as *"has it turned up?"* not *"have you looked?"* — and come with the replacement plan in your pocket so a "no" is a 2-minute item, not a moment.
- Housing: lead with your own timeline transparency before asking about hers; reciprocity follows.
- If only three things get done: calendar through Dec 31, holiday schedule, and activity decisions — those have real deadlines. Everything in Part 1 can be finished over text afterward.
- Bring the calendar (phone or printed) — "finalize" only happens if you can enter dates on the spot.
