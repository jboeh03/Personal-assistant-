## ⚠️ The one irreversible risk: wire fraud (§33)

**Before any money moves, call Fidelity Land Title on a number you look up yourself** —
not one from an email, not a number in a "corrected wiring instructions" message. Wire
fraud in real estate closings is the single unrecoverable loss in this entire process, and
it happens on closing morning, to people who are busy and distracted. If anyone emails
changed instructions today, treat it as fraud until a phone call proves otherwise.
