## 2. Deadline mechanics — read this box twice

## 7. Why (f)/termination is off the table — permanently

Refusing all corrections saves $0–$10,200 of concession and costs **−$12K to −$30K** (council-adjusted BATNA) **plus $5K–$13K of carry** (2–3.5 months at ~$2,500–$3,500/mo), through a separation, with the $10K earnest frozen at Fidelity and a mandatory ORC 5302.30 disclosure of *every now-known defect* — radon 13.6, roof, foundation movement, panel, HVAC — to a thinner, more skittish buyer pool on relist. That's roughly **3–8× the cost of any concession** on this table. Even the floor is soft: silence on 08/10 = deemed agreement to all repairs (~$5.5–10K worst case), which *still* beats walking. There is no branch of this decision tree where refusal wins. It is not a negotiating posture; it is a self-inflicted loss.
