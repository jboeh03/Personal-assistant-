## DO NOT SAY (any branch, any channel — calls, texts, emails)

- **No admissions about roof condition beyond the inspection report.** Do not characterize the roof as "storm-damaged," "failing," or "needing replacement" — the report's findings are the entire agreed factual record, and extra words become disclosure/claim material.
- **No open-ended repair promises.** Never "we'll take care of whatever needs doing" or "whatever the roofer finds." Scope is exactly what the signed writing says — nothing more.
- **No verbal-only agreements.** §28: modifications are invalid "unless in writing and signed by all parties." A phone-call handshake on 08/11 that isn't papered by end of 08/12 is an auto-terminated contract, not a deal.
- **No commitments on dates or dollars** beyond what's in the signed branch language — especially no promised install dates before the radon installer has confirmed one.
- **No discussion of the insurance angle** with the buyers' side; it is a seller-side cost-offset question only.
