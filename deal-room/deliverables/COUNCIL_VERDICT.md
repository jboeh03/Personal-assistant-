# Council Verdict — Defect Notice Response Decision

> ⚠️ Strategy analysis, not legal advice — review with your agent and an Ohio attorney before signing.

**Decision question:** Which Defect Notice response do the sellers deliver by end of Monday 08/10?
**Convened:** 2026-08-07 · Voices: Skeptic, Pragmatist, Critic (fresh-context subagents) vs. the strategist's H2 recommendation (radon pre-closing + roof credit, fallback (b)-amended).

## Consensus

All three voices agree on the frame, and it changes the recommendation from a fixed pick to a **gated sequence**:

1. **The Saturday call decides, not the matrix.** The single most decision-relevant, obtainable-before-Sunday item is a free ~10-minute call: **Amy temperature-checks Zach West (buyers' agent) on money-in-lieu vs. seller-performed repairs.** Every voice independently converged here. H2 is only the right opener if that call confirms the buyers will take a credit; the buyers *literally wrote* "Seller to have roofing contractor … prior to closing," and countering their stated preference on its weakest evidentiary leg is the plan's biggest exposure (Skeptic, Critic).
2. **If buyers signal "we want the repairs done":** open with **(b)-amended** — agree to perform everything, with a one-sentence initialed amendment that (i) pins the RS-2 sheathing scope ("repair affected sheathing at the three identified locations"), (ii) commits radon to an ODH-licensed contractor with retest <4.0 before re-inspection, and (iii) receipts per the notice. Acceptance-of-repairs paths carry **zero auto-termination risk** — §15 ends on acceptance; every counter, including H2, hands the buyers a 08/12 walk-away option (Critic's decisive point).
3. **If buyers signal money is fine:** deliver **H2** — initialed amendment: radon mitigation pre-closing (licensed, retest <4.0) + **$5,500** closing-cost credit in lieu of the roof/gutter package via option-(d) mechanics (top of the researched roof+gutter most-likely band; a credit is a cap, not a bid — no quotes needed on a weekend) (Pragmatist).
4. **Mechanics regardless of branch:** both sellers aligned by phone Saturday, e-signature packet ready Sunday noon (separating co-sellers never need to share a room), deliver **Monday afternoon** — not 11 PM; the Settlement Period (ends 08/12) is for signatures and lender confirmation only, never live negotiation.
5. **(f) stays buried** even after the Critic's BATNA haircut (realistic −$12K to −$30K, not −$20–45K — stigma and defect-extraction partially double-count): refusal risks 3–8× the cost of any concession.

## Strongest dissent

**Skeptic:** even the gated version under-prices the **appraisal channel**. H2 leaves visibly wind-creased shingles and buckled sheathing unrepaired when the appraiser visits (~08/14–21); a "subject to repairs" appraisal voids the credit as a remedy and forces pre-closing repairs anyway — starting ~08/21 with the contractor window already burned. (b)-amended is the only option that hedges this for free. If the Saturday call is ambiguous, this tips the opener to (b)-amended.

**Pragmatist (dissent on the fallback):** if the buyers want repairs but radon can't be booked in time, the fallback should be pure (d) money, **not** (b) — (b) reopens an August contractor race the sellers can't win from a weekend standing start.

## Premise check

- Cost bands are honestly labeled but **not quote-grade** (snippet-sourced, sites egress-blocked). Good enough to bound exposure and size a capped credit; the first real roofer quote supersedes everything (Critic).
- "Committed buyers" is EVIDENCE (over-ask, $10K earnest, $10K gap, waived big-ticket items). "Committed buyers will take cash instead of repairs" is HOPE until the Saturday call converts it to evidence (Critic).
- The matrix's six equal axes double-weight money (cost ≈ net proceeds) and under-weight termination asymmetry: acceptance ends §15 risk; every counter creates it (Critic — accepted by synthesis).
- **The floor is (a), not death:** silence on 08/10 = deemed agreement to all repairs (~$5.5–10K worst case), which still beats BATNA by a wide margin. Missing the deadline is expensive, not fatal — but it forfeits all the optimization above for nothing (Pragmatist).
