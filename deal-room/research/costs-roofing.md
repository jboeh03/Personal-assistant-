### Evidence detail per item

**RS-1 — shingle repair.** EVIDENCE: shingle roof repair typically $350–$1,900 avg ~$1,150; $4–$8/sq ft; a few shingles from ~$150; one square $500–$1,500 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-to-repair-asphalt-shingles.htm)). Minor wind-damage repair usually under $1,000 ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/roofing/asphalt-shingle-roof-repair)); corroborated by [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/home-ownership/home-improvement/learn/cost-to-replace-roof-shingles). INFERENCE: one slope with scattered creased shingles is sub-$1,500 in almost every sourced scenario; low end holds only when bundled. Band: **$350–$1,500**.

**RS-2 — sheathing.** EVIDENCE: decking replacement $70–$100/sheet installed; $2–$5/sq ft overall; labor $1.50–$3.00/sq ft ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/cost-to-replace-roof-decking)); ~$95/sheet as add-on, ~$150/sheet for few-sheet-only jobs ([MyHomePros](https://myhomepros.com/roofing/how-much-does-it-cost-to-replace-roof-decking/)); materials $22–$68/sheet ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/roof-sheathing-cost.htm)). INFERENCE (derivation): ≥3 locations ≈ 3–6 sheets → decking $300–$900 + tear-off/re-shingle ~1–2 squares ($500–$1,500) → composite **$800–$2,500**. Real risk: opening the roof reveals more bad decking than the attic view showed — the high end is not a ceiling.

**RS-3 — kick-out flashing.** EVIDENCE: single kick-out retrofit $250–$400; ~$935 where siding had to be removed/replaced ([RoofingTalk](https://www.roofingtalk.com/threads/retro-fitting-kick-out-flashing-how-hard-is-it-to-diy.11931/)); service minimums $110–$175, material $10–$32/unit ([JustAnswer](https://www.justanswer.com/home-improvement/nu86u-cost-install-flashing-home.html), [Family Handyman](https://www.familyhandyman.com/project/repair-siding-use-a-kick-out-flashing-to-stop-rot/)); minor flashing repair $200–$500 ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/roof-flashing-repair-cost)). INFERENCE: "transitions" plural + staining → assume 2–4 locations; bundled marginal cost $150–$250 each. Band: **$500–$1,600** (higher if siding rebuild needed — ties into EG-2).

**RS-6 — chimney crown + flashing.** EVIDENCE: crown coating/sealing $150–$400; partial repair $400–$1,000; full replacement $1,000–$2,500+ ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/cost-to-repair-replace-chimney-crown)); repair $150–$350, replacement to ~$3,000 ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/heating-and-cooling/chimney-repair), corroborated [Forbes Home](https://www.forbes.com/home-improvement/roofing/chimney-crown-repair-replacement-cost/)); chimney flashing $200–$850, fuller work $400–$1,600 ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-should-it-cost-replace-flashing-around-chimney.htm), [HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/chimney-repair-cost)). INFERENCE: "damaged… water penetrating" reads as more than hairline cracks — budget partial-repair-to-replacement, not sealant. Repair path **$350–$1,400**; replacement path **$1,500–$3,500**. Whether "repair" satisfies "repair/replace" is the buyers' call at re-inspection — **get the scope in writing before work starts**. Widest source spread of any item.

**EG-2 — wood siding.** EVIDENCE: wood siding repair $4–$13/sq ft ([HomeGuide](https://homeguide.com/costs/siding-repair-cost)); $9–$43/sq ft by wood type ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-to-repair-wood-siding.htm)); labor $2–$5/sq ft or $40–$90/hr ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/siding/wood-siding-repair)). INFERENCE: small loose section (<50 sq ft est.) at ladder height, 1–3 hours: **$150–$600 bundled; up to ~$750 standalone**.

## August scheduling reality (Cincinnati)

- EVIDENCE: summer is peak season; replacements schedule 2–4 weeks out, waits of 5+ weeks possible ([Trill Roofing](https://trillroofing.com/resources/process-and-scheduling/scheduling-a-roof-replacement/), [Topper Roofing](https://www.topperroofing.com/roofing/why-summer-is-peak-roofing-season-and-how-it-affects-pricing-availability/)). *Repairs* move much faster: small fixes commonly same-day once a crew arrives; storm/urgent repairs same/next-day slots ([Kind Roofer](https://kindroofer.com/typical-timelines-for-emergency-roof-repairs/), [Ryan Construction](https://www.ryanconstructionsystems.com/understand-roof-repair-timelines-how-long-does-it-take/)).
- INFERENCE: the binding constraint is **getting on the schedule**, not doing the work — the package is 1–2 crew-days. 08/10 agreement → 08/24 completion is **tight but realistic** if calls go out immediately (ideally 08/08, before the response is even filed), 3+ companies are contacted in parallel, and the sellers take whichever qualified firm commits in writing to completion by 08/22 (weather-slip margin before the 08/25 re-inspection). A single-contractor, wait-for-the-best-quote approach probably misses the window.
- RECOMMENDATION: Zach's contractor network is the fast path to a slot — but see next section on *who* signs the invoice.

## Agent-as-contractor note

- EVIDENCE: Ohio has **no state roofing license**; roofers are regulated municipally. The City of Cincinnati requires contractor registration, but 6840 Stonington is in **Anderson Township** — county/township rules apply ([ServiceTitan Ohio guide](https://www.servicetitan.com/licensing/roofing/ohio), [Adapt Digital — Ohio requirements](https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/articles/ohio-contractor-license-requirements/)).
- INFERENCE: "a professional qualified to make the agreed upon corrections" is a contract-interpretation question, not a licensing one. But the notice specifically says **"Seller to have roofing contractor"** for roof items and requires **work orders, receipts, warranties, proof of payment** at the 08/25 re-inspection. Receipts from the sellers' own agent wearing a contractor hat invite exactly the challenge you can't afford three days before closing — plus conflict-of-interest optics and a warranty that doesn't transfer like a roofing company's.
- INFERENCE (judgment call for the humans): defensible split — bona fide roofing company for RS-1/RS-2/RS-3 (+ chimney flashing), chimney/masonry company for the crown if the roofer declines, and the agent-contractor plausibly fine for EG-2 siding and gutter items where "roofing contractor" arguably doesn't attach. Run even that split past Zach and ideally float it to the buyers' agent before work starts.
- RECOMMENDATION: **Buy the certainty.** The package is likely under $7,500; the deal is $585,000. Do not risk the re-inspection over who held the ladder.
