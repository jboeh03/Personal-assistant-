## Evidence detail (EVIDENCE / INFERENCE labeled)

### Tier (a) — patch/seal
- EVIDENCE: crown coating/sealing $150–$400 ([HomeGuide crown](https://homeguide.com/costs/cost-to-repair-replace-chimney-crown)); professional crown sealing $175–$600 ([Allegiance Chimney](https://allegiancechimneysolutions.com/chimney-crown-repair/)); elastomeric crown sealant $200–$500 professionally, flexible crown coat $250–$500 ([Angi crown repair](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-chimney-crown-repair-cost.htm), [AM Roofing](https://amroofingandsiding.com/blogs/chimney-crown-repair-cost/)); crown *resurfacing* (thicker cracks) $800–$1,200 ([Angi crown repair](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-chimney-crown-repair-cost.htm)).
- EVIDENCE: basic chimney-flashing repair/reseal $200–$850 ([Angi flashing](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-should-it-cost-replace-flashing-around-chimney.htm), corroborated [HomeGuide chimney repair](https://homeguide.com/costs/chimney-repair-cost) — carried from v1).
- INFERENCE: seal $200–$600 + flashing reseal $200–$600 bundled → **$400–$1,200**.

### Tier (b) — full crown replacement (form + pour)
- EVIDENCE: completely failed crown requiring forming and pouring new concrete: **$1,200–$2,500** ([HomeGuide crown](https://homeguide.com/costs/cost-to-repair-replace-chimney-crown)); replacement $1,000–$3,000 total incl. labor and old-crown removal ([Angi crown repair](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-chimney-crown-repair-cost.htm)); full replacement $1,800–$3,000 where crown isn't salvageable ([AM Roofing](https://amroofingandsiding.com/blogs/chimney-crown-repair-cost/)); concrete is the most expensive crown material to repair/replace ([Forbes Home](https://www.forbes.com/home-improvement/roofing/chimney-crown-repair-replacement-cost/)).
- EVIDENCE (size-based contractor formula): full crown rebuild = (length" × width") + $1,500; **+ $2,000 for hard roof access** ([Brown Chimney crown](https://brownchimney.com/chimney-crown-repair-or-rebuild-cost)). Worked example: 36×36 crown ≈ $1,296 + $1,500 ≈ $2,800 standard access.
- EVIDENCE (Cincinnati anchor): crown repairs **$600–$1,800** locally ([Shamrock Chimney Cincinnati](https://www.shamrockchimney.com/cincinnati-oh-chimney-repair/) / Cincinnati chimney-services search excerpt); labor is 75–80% of crown job cost ([Forbes Home](https://www.forbes.com/home-improvement/roofing/chimney-crown-repair-replacement-cost/)); Cincinnati freeze-thaw cited as accelerating crown deterioration ([Chimney Works Cincinnati](https://www.chimneyworksonline.com/services/chimney-repairs/chimney-crown-repair)).
- INFERENCE (derivation): national pour band $1,200–$3,000 → multi-flue oversized crown (Brown formula) and 2-story access loading (below) push the top up; + flashing correction $200–$850 → **$1,600–$4,000**.

### Access / scaffolding premium (question 3)
- EVIDENCE: two-story access adds ~15%; three-story or scaffold setup adds 30%+; scaffold setup alone $500–$1,500 on steep-pitch/3-story before repair begins ([Full Service Chimney](https://fullservicechimney.com/how-much-does-chimney-repair-cost/), [HomeGuide chimney repair](https://homeguide.com/costs/chimney-repair-cost)); steep roofs add 20–40% ([Sunrise Roofing & Chimney](https://sunriseroofingandchimney.com/chimney-repair-cost-in-long-island-a-homeowners-guide/)); a low outlier says scaffold +$100–$200 ([firepitsurplus calculator](https://www.firepitsurplus.com/blogs/calculator/chimney-repair-cost-calculator)).
- EVIDENCE: Brown Chimney prices access as a flat formula bump (+$500 over standard for hard access on repair; +$2,000 tier on rebuild) rather than a separate scaffold invoice line ([Brown Chimney](https://brownchimney.com/chimney-crown-repair-or-rebuild-cost)).
- INFERENCE (answer): Cincinnati chimney companies mostly **bake access into the quote** (percentage or flat bump) rather than billing scaffolding separately; expect the tall-2-story premium to appear as roughly **+15–40% / +$300–$1,500** inside the number, and *ask each bidder to state access assumptions in the quote* so bids compare cleanly.

### Tier (c) — crown + top-course rebuild
- EVIDENCE: partial rebuild of the top few feet/courses: $600–$1,000 low case ([HomeGuide rebuild](https://homeguide.com/costs/chimney-rebuild-cost)); partial chimney rebuild $1,500–$4,000 ([HomeAdvisor rebuild](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/heating-and-cooling/chimney-rebuild/)); advanced stack repairs incl. partial rebuilds above roofline $2,000–$6,000 ([Angi chimney repair](https://www.angi.com/articles/what-should-it-cost-replace-and-rebuild-masonry-chimney.htm)); stack work ≈ $150/linear ft; brick replacement $60–$200/linear ft ([Angi 2-story chimney](https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-replace-2-story-chimney.htm), [HomeGuide rebuild](https://homeguide.com/costs/chimney-rebuild-cost)).
- EVIDENCE: rebuild quotes include new crown, flashing, and cap as required line items ([Brown Chimney rebuild](https://brownchimney.com/chimney-rebuild-cost)).
- INFERENCE: 2–4 courses on a tall multi-flue stack + form/pour crown + flashing, 2-story access → **$2,500–$6,000**. This is the single most common "the quote grew after they got up there" outcome; budget against it, don't be surprised by it.

### Tier (d) — surprise ceiling / EG-1 escalation
- EVIDENCE: full chimney rebuilds above the roofline in Cincinnati **$5,000–$15,000+** depending on height ([Shamrock Chimney Cincinnati](https://www.shamrockchimney.com/cincinnati-oh-chimney-repair/) excerpt).
- EVIDENCE (tuckpointing): Cincinnati tuckpointing $400–$1,200 (same local excerpt); chimney tuckpointing typically $500–$2,500, avg ~$1,200, $8–$30/sq ft ([Angi tuckpointing](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-tuckpointing-cost.htm), [HomeGuide tuckpointing](https://homeguide.com/costs/repointing-or-tuckpointing-cost)); above-8-ft work +20–40% for scaffolding; two-story chimney tuckpointing $1,500–$3,500 ([HomeGuide tuckpointing](https://homeguide.com/costs/repointing-or-tuckpointing-cost), [North Shore Brickwork](https://northshorebrickwork.com/masonry/how-much-does-tuckpointing-cost-per-square-foot-in-milwaukee/)).
- INFERENCE: EG-1 (cracked veneer/mortar) was **waived** — the buyers have no contractual hook to demand it under this notice, and F-2's CSIA level-2 flue inspection was recommended but **not demanded**. The risk is soft: a re-inspection where the same inspector sees fresh crown work next to untouched cracked masonry invites a late push. Planning ceiling **$8,000** (extensive upper rebuild + modest tuckpointing); the $15,000+ full-stack figure is real but exceeds any defensible reading of "repair/replace crown/cap and correct flashing" — treat any demand at that level as a negotiation event, not a repair order.
- RECOMMENDATION: do **not** volunteer a level-2 flue inspection or veneer tuckpointing; both open discovery paths (flue liner condition on a 1974 multi-flue stack) that the notice does not require before 08/28.

## Repair-vs-replace ambiguity & scope lock (question 4)

- EVIDENCE: the notice remedy is "repair/**replace** chimney crown/cap and correct flashing"; work must be "completed in a professional, workmanlike manner by a professional qualified to make the agreed upon corrections," with work orders, warranties, receipts, and proof of payment at the ~08/25 re-inspection ([inspection-findings.md](../source/inspection-findings.md)).
- INFERENCE: **tier (a) is challengeable** — a brush coat over a crown the report calls "damaged" with water "currently" penetrating gives the buyers' inspector an easy fail. **Tier (b) is the defensible satisfaction point**: old crown removed, new cast-in-place concrete crown with proper overhang/drip edge, flashing corrected, documented by a chimney/masonry company with photos and a warranty. Nothing in the notice's text requires tier (c) unless the crew's own findings do.
- RECOMMENDATION (lock the scope in writing, before work starts): have the agents paper a one-paragraph addendum or email confirmation stating that *"full replacement of the concrete chimney crown (cast-in-place, with overhang/drip edge) and correction of chimney flashing, performed by [named chimney/masonry company], satisfies Defect Notice item RS-6,"* and explicitly that **EG-1 veneer/mortar items remain waived and outside RS-6**. Require the contractor's before/after photos in the closing package. This converts the re-inspection from a judgment call into a checklist item and caps the EG-1 escalation vector.
- RECOMMENDATION (trade selection, carried from v1): this is masonry — a chimney/masonry company receipt is the clean answer; the roofer can do the flashing if the chimney company won't, but keep the crown off the roofer's and off the agent-as-contractor's invoice.

## Schedule feasibility — 08/11 agreement → 08/24 completion (question 5)

- EVIDENCE: the chimney "fall rush" begins in the **latter half of August/early September**; peak-season waits run 1–3+ weeks; industry advice is to book in August to beat the October rush ([Smart Service](https://www.smartservice.com/smart-service-blog/handle-the-chimney-sweep-busy-season/), [Bella FSM](https://www.bellafsm.com/chimney-sweep-busy-season/), [Southern Chimneys](https://southernchimneys.com/beat-the-winter-rush-tips-on-when-to-schedule-your-fireplace-and-chimney-cleaning/)).
- EVIDENCE (cure sequencing): a newly poured crown needs **~48 hours cure** before crown-saver/waterproof coating is applied; brushable sealers then cure in 2–4 hours, trowelable in 24–72 hours ([Rockford Chimney Supply — CrownCoat](https://www.rockfordchimneysupply.com/products/chimney-saver-crown-coat-brushable-sealant), [Lindemann Chimney Supply](https://lindemannchimneysupply.com/crowncoat-brushable-sealant)).
- INFERENCE (feasibility): tier (b)/(c) is **1–2 site days + a 48-hour cure + a short return visit** — roughly 4–6 calendar days end-to-end. Flashing work does not wait on the crown cure. Booked by ~08/14–08/15, the work finishes with margin before 08/22 (the weather-slip buffer v1 set ahead of the 08/25 re-inspection). **The window works; the queue is the risk, and Cincinnati is entering exactly the season where the queue forms.**
- RECOMMENDATION: call **3+ Cincinnati chimney companies on 08/08–08/11 in parallel** (Chimney Works, A1 Chimney/Cincinnati Chimney Services, Vonderhaar, Cincinnati Chimney & Masonry, Shamrock, Oberer, Best Cincinnati Chimney all serve the area), ask each to quote tier (b) with a stated per-course adder for any top-course rebuild found on tear-off, and take the first qualified firm that commits in writing to completion by 08/22.
