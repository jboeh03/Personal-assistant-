## Seller response options (§15(b) + Defect Notice form)

| Option | Mechanic | Note |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Agree to complete all corrections as requested | Binding; ends negotiation; work must be done pre-closing by qualified pros |
| (b) | Offer to complete corrections **as amended** (initialed edits) | Counteroffer → starts 2-day Settlement Period |
| (c) | Price reduction of $____ in lieu of corrections | Counteroffer |
| (d) | Pay buyer settlement charges/closing costs up to $____ in lieu | Counteroffer; capped by what buyer's lender allows |
| (e) | Title-company checks to named contractor(s) at closing for post-closing work; escrow if lender/seller requires | Counteroffer; keeps 08/28 date safest; lender acceptance is the open question |
| (f) | Refuse all corrections | Counteroffer; buyer accepting = waives listed items |

Any of (b)–(f) ends the Consideration Period and starts the 2-day Settlement Period. **If no mutual signed agreement by end of Settlement Period, the contract automatically terminates.**
